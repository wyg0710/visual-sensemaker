# Visual Routing

Choose the visual structure from the relationship the user needs to understand, not from stylistic preference.

| Information structure | Primary visual | Good signals | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steps, states, or decisions | Flowchart | then, if, approval, process, lifecycle | Exact timing is the main message |
| Events ordered in time | Timeline | dates, milestones, phases, history | Order is logical rather than temporal |
| Alternatives with shared criteria | Comparison matrix or cards | versus, pros and cons, options, features | The items do not share comparable criteria |
| Parent-child or part-whole relationships | Hierarchy or tree | categories, organization, taxonomy, components | Cross-links dominate the structure |
| Interconnected ideas | Concept map | causes, influences, themes, relationships | A strict sequence is more important |
| Quantitative patterns | Data chart | trend, distribution, correlation, ranking | Values are absent or incomparable |

## Quantitative Chart Routing

- Change over an ordered variable: line chart.
- Compare discrete categories: bar chart, normally starting at zero.
- Relationship between two quantitative variables: scatter plot.
- Distribution: histogram, box plot, or dot plot depending on sample size and audience.
- Part-to-whole: stacked bar; use a pie chart only for a few positive parts that sum to a meaningful whole.

Do not turn identifiers or unordered categories into a line. Do not use area or volume to encode values unless the area calculation is correct and necessary.

## Multi-panel Rule

Use multiple panels only if a single structure cannot preserve the relationships. Give each panel one job and connect panels through consistent labels, ordering, and color. If the result needs more than four panels, consider producing a short series instead of one overloaded visual.

## Ambiguity

When two structures fit, choose the one that best answers the user's stated question. If the question is unstated, choose the structure that introduces the least interpretation and mention the choice in the handoff.
